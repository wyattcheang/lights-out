Race rules in this game
  • Energy. The MGU-K adds up to 350 kW. Normal deployment fades above 290 km/h. The battery recharges under braking, lift-and-coast and clipping at the end of straights.
  • Overtake mode. Within 1.0 s of the car ahead at the detection point earns +0.5 MJ and full power to 337 km/h for the next lap.
  • Active aero. Wings open into Straight mode on the long straights and close when you brake or turn in. Disabled under caution.
  • Tyres. Softs are fastest and wear quickest. In a dry race you must use two different compounds or be disqualified.
  • Pit lane. 80 km/h limiter, about 2.5 s stationary. Unserved time penalties are served at your stop.
  • Caution. No overtaking under yellow, VSC or Safety Car: give the place back within 8 s or take 5 s. Under VSC keep the delta positive.
  • Safety Car. Queue behind it. After "Safety Car in this lap" the leader controls the restart; racing resumes at the line.
  • Track limits. Two warnings, then black-and-white flag, then 5 s. Points go 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.
